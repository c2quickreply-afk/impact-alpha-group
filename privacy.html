What we collect, and why

  • Contact form. Your name, email address and message. We use them only to read and reply to your message.
  • Newsletter signup. When you join The Impact Earnings Dispatch, the signup page asks for your name, email address and phone number, and offers an optional box to receive text messages. We use this to send the newsletter, and to text you only if you checked that box.
  • Community Call registration. To register for the call, you give your name, email address and, on some registration pages, your phone number. We use this to send you the call details and reminders.
  • Booking a call with Francene. The booking calendar collects the time you choose, your time zone, and the contact details you enter, so we can hold the conversation.
  • Basic technical information. Like nearly every website, our hosting provider automatically records information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

These two websites do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or other browser storage. The fonts are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn’t send your information to a font provider.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and these services. They handle your information on our behalf:

  • Vercel, which hosts this website. Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • FormSubmit, which delivers contact-form messages to our email. FormSubmit’s privacy policy.
  • Google Workspace, our email provider, where messages and replies are stored. Google’s privacy policy.
  • HighLevel (LeadConnector), which runs our signup, registration and booking pages at go.impactearnings.com and stores what you enter there. Those pages may also set cookies of their own, including a security cookie from Cloudflare. HighLevel’s privacy policy.
  • Zoom, which hosts the Community Call and its registration page. Zoom’s privacy policy.

Links to LinkedIn and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don’t share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link. If you agreed to text messages, reply STOP to any text to stop them.
  • To ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email francene@impactearnings.com. We’ll answer within 30 days.

Children

Our websites and services are not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we’ll delete it.
